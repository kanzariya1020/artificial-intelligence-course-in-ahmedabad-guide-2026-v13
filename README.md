Understanding Artificial Intelligence: The Basics

Before delving into specific courses, it’s essential to grasp the fundamentals of AI. At its core, artificial intelligence refers to systems or machines that mimic human cognitive functions such as learning and problem-solving. As a developer, you will encounter various AI subsets, including:


    Machine Learning: Algorithms that allow computers to learn from data and improve over time without being explicitly programmed.
    Natural Language Processing (NLP): Techniques for enabling machines to understand and interpret human language.
    Computer Vision: The ability of machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world.
    Robotics: The branch of technology that deals with the design, construction, operation, and use of robots.
    Deep Learning: A subset of machine learning that uses neural networks to analyze various factors of data.
    Reinforcement Learning: An area of machine learning where an agent learns to make decisions by performing actions and receiving feedback.
